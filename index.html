Frequently asked

Questions worth answering upfront.

Can you fix my leaking roof?+
Almost certainly. Book a free assessment and we'll come back with a straight quote.
Do you work on tile or slate roofs?+
We cover most roof typesFibre cement and asbestos-free sheeting, corrugated iron and IBR, natural slate, concrete tile, low-pitch tile, thatch conversions, flat concrete slabs with torch-on waterproofing, and asphalt shingles.
How long have you been in business?+
Since 1985. Three generations of the same family, still hands-on in the business today.

Not within our main service area. A call-out fee may apply if you're further out.
